# Algebra findings

Across 8 algebra worlds, eight training vectors gave mean normalized squared errors of 2.4e-31 and 0.962 for the matched and mismatched canonical-sensor fits. On randomized sensors those errors were 2e-31 and 0.943. With 2,048 input pairs, the blind bilinear learner recovered at least 1,024 of the 1,024 signed basis products per world. Its mean error was 1.9e-30, compared with 2.06 after shuffling targets.

The multiplier and blind bilinear fits isolate coefficient estimation. The neural scene study additionally requires noisy sensor decoding, spatial selection and comparing two objects.
