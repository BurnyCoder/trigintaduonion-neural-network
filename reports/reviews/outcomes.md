# Outcome review

The registered crossover is complete. Reversing the observation rule reversed which of two identically sized neural networks generalized to fresh scenes. The matched cases also retained accuracy on the withheld sensor channels and on scenes with more candidate objects. This is a controlled intervention in a simulator with an encoded channel relationship. The learned quantity under test is a spatial relation between two latent object types. A model trained in one world is evaluated on fresh families in that world. The unit of replication is a world. Each world changes the hidden filter, randomized table and training seed. Treating the two mates, individual objects, or individual model decisions as independent replicates would underestimate uncertainty. An independent recount reconstructs family accuracy and classification accuracy from individual decisions. The fit and evaluation manifests establish that all registered primary and control checkpoints were fixed before opening the first confirmatory test outcomes.
