# What the observation rule makes learnable

This supplementary calculation was written after the development pilot and before the confirmatory test outcomes were opened. It gives a reference prediction for the registered simulator. [Protocol](../preregistration.md) · [Renderer and oracle](../../src/trig32/scenes.py)

For either sign table, fix a unit filter $w$ and a queried channel $k$. The coefficients of the row $L(w)_{k,:}$ are a signed permutation of $w$. Its squared norm is one even though the full left-multiplication matrix need not be orthogonal. With $x$ standard Gaussian, the clean response $s=L(w)_{k,:}x$ is therefore standard Gaussian. Both sensor rules have the same marginal signal power at every channel. For either type $b$, the observation $z=bs+\epsilon$ alone has the same centered Gaussian distribution. Type information is carried by the relationship between the excitation, query and observation. [Basis routing](../../src/trig32/reference.py) · [Noise generation](../../src/trig32/scenes.py)

Conditional on $x,k,b$, the scalar $z$ has mean $bs$. The log likelihood ratio between the two types is $2sz/\sigma^2$. The implemented privileged control knows $s$ and uses the sign of $sz$ at each selected object. Under the isotropic Gaussian law, the probability of one sign error is $q=\arctan(\sigma)/\pi$. To see this, write the sign test as agreement between the projections of a two-dimensional standard Gaussian onto $(1,0)$ and $(1,\sigma)$. The angle between these directions gives the disagreement probability. [Goemans and Williamson, Lemma 3.2](https://math.mit.edu/~goemans/PAPERS/maxcut-jacm.pdf)

The two selected objects are distinct and have independent excitations and noise, even conditional on their types and geometric selections. Their equality decision is correct if both single-object signs are right or both are wrong. The oracle's population accuracy is $(1-q)^2+q^2$, for the noise level specified in the protocol. Swapping a complete reading with an opposite-type distractor preserves that expectation. The empirical oracle entries in the results table use the actual sampled scenes. This formula describes the implemented two-sign control. A joint decoder could also exploit the generator's fixed global type counts. [Measured outcomes](../summary.md)

The encoder has a route to the signal because it learns projected excitations, queries them at $k$ and forms products with $z$. It must learn these projections solely from scene equality labels. Reversing the sign convention for every inferred type preserves every equality label, so that supervision cannot uniquely identify the sign of the simulated filter. Each original/mate pair has identical geometry and identical multiset of reading bundles. A classifier given either of those summaries alone makes the same decision on both members and is correct on exactly half of them.
