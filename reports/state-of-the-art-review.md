# Methods and reuse · 26 November 2026

The proposed contribution is a crossed intervention on the observation rule of a two-hop scene task. Arbitrary-dimensional hypercomplex layers already exist. Bojesomo's implementation constructs multiplication-component matrices recursively. The independent MIT-licensed Python package hypercomplex implements the fifth doubling as CD32. Use the scalar package for an independent basis-table oracle. Use indexed signed real kernels for GPU training. [General neural layers](https://github.com/bojesomo/hypercomplex-network) · [Scalar reference source](https://github.com/discretegames/hypercomplex/blob/d026ee205a4bf41f93b5c67df17e212ca3be5de2/hypercomplex/hypercomplex.py)

Grassucci et al. learn mixing rules through parameterized hypercomplex convolutions. Here both competing rules are fixed in advance so the change in performance can be attributed to their compatibility with the generator under the registered training procedure. A recent audit also motivates parameter-matched structured controls. Its per-task evidence does not establish an outcome for the present study. [PHNN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.04176) · [Audit and source data](https://github.com/mxkuzn/hypercomplex-placebo-audit)

Dwivedi et al. benchmark CIFAR-10 superpixel graphs with mean colors, spatial coordinates and a nearest-neighbor graph. The public parquet mirror preserves graph splits. Barz and Denzler replaced identified near duplicates in the original CIFAR test sets. Scores on original CIFAR-10 and ciFAIR-10 share many images. [GNN benchmark](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-0567.html) · [Superpixels mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/graphs-datasets/CIFAR10) · [CIFAR](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html) · [ciFAIR](https://cvjena.github.io/cifair/)
